How it works

Three steps, no dispatcher in between

01

Search or publish

Tell us where you're headed, or list your own empty seats with your route and time.

02

Pick a match

Compare departure times, price per seat, and the vehicle before you commit.

03

Book the seat, meet at the pickup point, and split the cost of a trip you were both already making.

Why RideMitra

Built for the trip you're already taking

Real routes, not detours

Rides are listed by the drivers actually making that trip — no rerouting to pick up strangers across town.

You set the price

Drivers price a seat to cover fuel and tolls, not to run a taxi service. No surge, no platform cut.

Fast to publish, fast to book

A ride goes live in under a minute. Booking one takes a single tap and a confirmation.
